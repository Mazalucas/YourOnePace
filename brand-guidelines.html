Your One Pace Brand Guidelines

This guide defines how to present Your One Pace with consistency across product, social media, and community touchpoints. It translates the existing visual system into practical rules for professional execution.

Brand Foundation

Your One Pace is a premium, fan-centered utility designed for clarity and momentum. The brand should always feel focused, efficient, and respectful of the source community.

  • Positioning: A modern progress tracker for the One Pace journey.
  • Voice: Clear, supportive, concise, and confident.
  • Tone: Premium but approachable, never noisy or overhyped.
  • Promise: Fast orientation, clean progression, no clutter.

Logo System

YOUR ONE PACE

The primary logotype is a three-part wordmark using Outfit in uppercase. It relies on color hierarchy to emphasize identity: gold for the opener and accent red for the core brand term.

  • Clear space: Keep at least the height of “ONE” around all sides.
  • Minimum size: 120px width for digital use to preserve legibility.
  • Background: Use dark surfaces for default version; avoid patterned backgrounds.
  • Lockup: Do not condense, stretch, rotate, or apply effects not defined in this guide.

Typography System

Outfit 700 / Headline system

Inter 400-700 / Interface and long-form copy. Keep text contrast high, avoid all-caps in long paragraphs, and maintain generous spacing for readability.

  • Headings: Outfit, 700-800 weight.
  • Body copy: Inter, 400-600 weight.
  • UI labels: Inter 600+ with concise copy.
  • Density: Prefer breathable spacing over compressed layouts.

UI Principles

Clarity First

Primary actions remain obvious and visually prioritized in every section.

Depth With Purpose

Glass surfaces and blur are used to organize hierarchy, never as decoration only.

Progress Visibility

Progress indicators should be explicit, easy to scan, and semantically consistent.

Do and Don’t

Do

  • Use brand tokens for every foreground/background pairing.
  • Maintain logo clear space and scale proportionally.
  • Use accent red for interaction priority and call-to-actions.
  • Keep copy concise and functional.

Don’t

  • Use low-contrast gray text over glass cards.
  • Apply extra gradients or neon effects to the wordmark.
  • Swap font families outside Outfit and Inter.
  • Overload pages with decorative elements that reduce legibility.
